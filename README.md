Marketing Portfolio

Welcome to my Marketing Portfolio, a collection of practical projects showcasing my knowledge, creativity and developing skills in Marketing Management and Digital Marketing.

I hold a National Diploma in Marketing Management from Central Johannesburg TVET College and have practical experience in marketing administration, communication and organisational support.

This repository serves as a platform to showcase my ability to develop marketing concepts, create digital content, communicate brand messages and apply marketing strategies to different business environments.

The portfolio includes projects covering:

- Digital Marketing: Developing digital campaigns and promotional content to increase brand awareness.
- Social Media Marketing: Creating social media advertisements, Instagram content and audience engagement concepts.
- Campaign Development: Identifying campaign objectives, target audiences, communication channels and marketing approaches.
- Brand Communication: Exploring visual identity, promotional messaging, colour combinations and brand consistency.
- Content Creation: Designing marketing materials, posters, presentations and digital advertisements.
- Marketing Analytics: Exploring campaign performance indicators, engagement metrics and data-driven marketing decisions.

Projects are developed using tools such as Canva, Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint, alongside other digital learning resources.

One of my featured projects is Banking Without Boundaries, a fictional digital banking campaign developed under the NovaBank brand. It demonstrates campaign planning, social media design, promotional communication and target audience identification.

My objective is to continuously improve my practical marketing abilities, build creative confidence and gain exposure to real-world marketing challenges.

This portfolio reflects my commitment to continuous learning, professional development and exploring innovative ways to connect brands with their audiences.
