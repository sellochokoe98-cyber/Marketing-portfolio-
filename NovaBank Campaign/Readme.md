Digital Marketing Poster – Smartphone Banking Campaign

Project Overview

This project focuses on creating a digital marketing poster promoting a modern smartphone banking experience. The aim is to communicate convenience, accessibility and the benefits of managing everyday finances through digital banking.

Design Approach

The poster uses a clean, professional layout to attract attention and communicate the campaign message effectively.

The design follows these principles:

- Main headline: Large, bold lettering to make the campaign message stand out.
- Description: Smaller text to provide supporting information without overcrowding the poster.
- Gold accents: Important words and key messages are highlighted in gold to attract attention.
- White supporting text: Used to maintain readability and contrast against the dark background.
- Smartphone imagery: A smartphone mockup or image of a person using a smartphone is included to represent digital banking and modern technology.

Target Audience

Young adults and working professionals who use smartphones and are interested in convenient digital financial services.

Marketing Objective

To increase awareness of digital banking by presenting it as a simple, accessible and convenient way to manage everyday finances.

Tools

- Canva – Poster design and layout
- AI-assisted imagery – Visual concept development

Skills Demonstrated

- Promotional poster design
- Visual branding
- Content development
- Target audience identification
- Marketing message development

Project Note

This is a fictional marketing concept created for portfolio and learning purposes. It is not an official advertisement for a real financial institution.

The project demonstrates my developing practical skills in digital marketing and creative brand communication.
